## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-luxury, romantic, and ceremonial presence designed explicitly for formal celebrations, bespoke weddings, and milestone galas. The visual language evokes tactile artisanal stationery, vintage botanical foil stamping, and classic editorial typography, seamlessly adapted for modern mobile card interactions.

The target audience comprises discerning couples, event hosts, and invited guests who value intentionality, timeless craft, and reverent beauty. Every touchpoint feels like opening an exquisite heavyweight cotton envelope lined with silk and stamped in pressed metallic foil.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Elegance** with **Tactile Skeuomorphic Warmth**:
- Generous, stately vertical rhythm with intentional open space mimicking artisanal paper margins.
- Delicate hairline metallic accents, botanical corner motifs, and filigree framing.
- Rich textural balance between solemn deep evergreen hues, shimmering metallic gold, and soft candlelit cream backgrounds.

## Colors

The color palette centers on ceremonial heritage and golden hour warmth:

- **Primary (`#0B3D2E`) — Deep Emerald**: Serves as the anchor color for primary call-to-action buttons, monograms, and solemn typographic highlights. It grounds the design in timeless prestige.
- **Secondary (`#C9A227`) — Antique Gold**: Applied to hairline filigree borders, foil-like badges, active selection rings, and ornamental flourishes.
- **Tertiary (`#8A6D1C`) — Burnished Bronze**: A deeper, high-contrast gold derivative used for micro-copy labels, dates, icons, and subtle ornamental dividers where accessibility requires strong legibility against pale surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6EC`) — Pale Ivory**: The foundational canvas color evoking heavy cold-press cotton stationery. All surface layers, cards, and modal sheets stem from this tone.
- **Text Charcoal (`#1F2421`)**: Deep botanical charcoal used for all long-form body copy, itinerary descriptions, and registry details to avoid the harshness of pure black while ensuring strict readability.
- **Gilded Borders & Highlights (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.35)` to `rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.75)`)**: Subtly luminous accent lines that structure sections without heavy graphic weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast luxury serif **Playfair Display** with the clear, balanced geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Used for couple names, ceremonial milestones, section overtures, and card title treatments. Tracking is deliberately relaxed (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to mirror letterpressed invitations. Always maintain optical center-alignment for hero headings.
- **Body & Functional Details (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers effortless legibility for venue directions, dress codes, schedule timelines, and RSVP field text.
- **Caps & Micro-metadata (`label-caps`)**: Always set in all-caps with generous character spacing (`0.14em`) and uppercase formatting. Used for dates, badges, status indicators, and RSVP deadlines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is tailored for vertical storytelling on portrait displays, framing the mobile screen as an interactive formal invitation folio:

- **Container Model**: On mobile viewports, the primary invitation surface adopts a card sheet centered inside the viewport with a fixed maximum width of `440px` on desktop/tablet views. The outer canvas exposes the Pale Ivory neutral base, while card layers use warm cream surfaces encased in subtle gold hairline outlines.
- **Rhythm & Grid**: Built upon an 8pt baseline scale. Gaps between distinct thematic sections (e.g., Hero to Schedule, Schedule to Accommodations, Accommodations to RSVP) scale to `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to maintain breathable, museum-grade composition.
- **Adaptive Rules**:
  - **Mobile (<600px)**: 1-column layout, edge margins of `1.25rem`, sticky floating pill button dock for RSVP at screen bottom.
  - **Tablet & Desktop (≥600px)**: Fixed-width card folio anchored in viewport center with `3rem` canvas margin and dual-column splits for schedule and event logistics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth emulates tactile, layered fine paper stocks illuminated by soft diffuse lighting:

- **Surface Layers**:
  - `Base Canvas`: Pale Ivory (`#FAF6EC`).
  - `Card Foil Folio`: Pure warm eggshell white (`#FFFDF9`) paired with a double-stroke perimeter: an outer subtle border (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.4)`) and an inner offset hairline border (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.25)`).
  - `Floating Overlays & Sheets`: Elevated RSVP drawers and dialogs utilize `#FFFDF9` with a delicate golden ambient halo.
- **Shadow Philosophy**: No harsh or pitch-black shadows. All shadows are tinted with Deep Emerald and warm umber tones:
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(11, 61, 46, 0.08), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.06)`
  - *Floating Pill Actions*: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(11, 61, 46, 0.24), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.12)`
  - *Input & Selected States*: Inner subtle glow using `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 61, 46, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces high-elegance curves and fluid pill forms:

- **Pill Architecture (`level 3`)**: Applied to all primary and secondary action triggers, attendance badges, and date flags, creating a refined and approachable touch signature.
- **Invitation Cards & Panels**: Use generous `rounded-xl` corners (`2rem` / `32px`) to mimic custom die-cut stationery edges.
- **Ornamental Frames**: Inner filigree borders reflect the card's outer radius with an offset margin of `8px` to maintain geometric harmony.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (Pill)**: Deep Emerald background (`#0B3D2E`), high-contrast white or soft cream typography (`#FAF6EC`), fine `1px` Antique Gold metallic border (`#C9A227`). Fully rounded pill silhouette (`9999px`), minimum height `52px` for comfortable thumb interaction.
- **Secondary Action (Ghost Pill)**: Transparent or soft cream background, `1.5px` border in Antique Gold (`#C9A227`), text in Deep Emerald. On press, fills with a translucent champagne wash (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.1)`).

### Chips & Badges
- Encased in full-pill shapes with uppercase `label-caps` typography.
- Neutral variant: Ivory background with antique gold border.
- Selected / Confirmed variant: Deep Emerald fill with antique gold lettering.

### Invitation Cards & Folios
- Centered cream background (`#FFFDF9`) surrounded by a dual-line gold perimeter framing botanical corner vignettes.
- Subtle inner divider: A centered ornamental gold diamond or botanical sprig glyph (`✦`) flanked by tapering hairline rules (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.35)`).

### Input Fields & Text Areas
- Built for RSVP forms (guest names, meal selections, dietary notes).
- Flat cream surface with `1px` muted gold outline (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.45)`), rounded to `1rem`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to solid Antique Gold (`#C9A227`) paired with a faint emerald-tinted ring (`rgba(11, 61, 46, 0.1)`).
- Floating placeholder labels utilize `label-md` in burnished bronze.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selection
- **Radio Buttons (Attendance / Entrée Selection)**: Circular with a double ring; active state fills with Deep Emerald containing a centered Antique Gold disc.
- **Checkboxes**: Smoothly rounded squares (`6px`) with Antique Gold borders and Deep Emerald fill with a crisp cream checkmark icon.

### Timeline & Itinerary Nodes
- Vertical connecting rule in delicate Antique Gold hairline (`1px`).
- Time markers set in bold uppercase `label-caps` alongside editorial serif event titles (`headline-sm`).
- Milestone icons housed in miniature circular cream medallions with gold borders.

### Wax Seal Badge & Floating RSVP Bar
- **Wax Seal Element**: A circular stamped emblem rendered in Deep Emerald or Gold foil relief used as an authentication signature or RSVP confirmation trigger.
- **Floating Bottom Bar**: Fixed mobile footer dock styled in frosted Pale Ivory (`rgba(250, 246, 236, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), housing the primary "RSVP Now" pill action.
